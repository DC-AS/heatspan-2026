Preventative Boiler Maintenance

Regular maintenance helps prepare your heating system for winter and gives a technician an opportunity to identify developing problems before they become emergency repairs.

Visual Inspection

Review visible controls, wiring, sheet-metal components, burner areas, piping, and signs of water leakage or soot.

Operating Check

Confirm that the system is cycling, heating, and responding as expected while looking for signs of abnormal operation.

Early Problem Detection

Identify unusual noises, pressure concerns, leaks, or deteriorated components before they cause a loss of heat.

Homeowner Maintenance Awareness

  • Keep the boiler area clear of flammable materials and airflow obstructions
  • Watch for visible water leaks
  • Pay attention to soot, unusual odors, or changes around the burner area
  • Do not ignore cold radiators, repeated cycling, or new noises
Safety: Working carbon-monoxide detectors and professional heating-system maintenance are important parts of home safety.

Prepare Before the Cold Weather Arrives

Scheduling maintenance before peak heating season can make it easier to address small issues before the system is under its heaviest workload.
