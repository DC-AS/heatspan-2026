Boiler Maintenance

Regular maintenance helps prepare your boiler for winter and can uncover developing problems before they cause an unexpected loss of heat.

CALL 718-375-3320REQUEST SERVICE

Maintenance for Peace of Mind

Heatspan recommends having the boiler inspected before the winter heating season. A technician can inspect the boiler and its components and identify conditions that need attention.

Warning Signs

  • Grumbling, gurgling or whistling sounds
  • Lack of hot water
  • Cold radiators
  • Boiler repeatedly switching on and off

Homeowner Awareness

Keep the boiler area clear of flammable materials and objects that can obstruct airflow. Visible water leaks, soot, deteriorated controls or wiring should be evaluated by a qualified technician.
