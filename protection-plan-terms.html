THE SERVICES WE PROVIDE AND COVER

The service contract is between the customer and HEATSPAN. This plan is available to residential homeowners located in one to three family houses. The plan provides coverage for (a) ONE gas fired central heating equipment with an hourly output no greater than 300,000 BTU’s covering no more than three heating zones. (b) one gas or electric water heating equipment with an hourly input no greater than 95,000 BTUs or (c) one gas fired hot water boiler 0r (d) one steam boiler. All equipment must meet state and local installation codes. HEATSPAN offers three different plans, each of which can be selected by checking the appropriate box (es) on the accompanying page.
