Comprehensive Heating Maintenance

Heatspan's maintenance service goes beyond a basic visual inspection. Technicians clean, inspect, test and adjust key heating-system components to support dependable operation, efficiency and safety. Heatspan also provides 24/7 emergency service when heating, cooling or ventilation trouble arises.

Cleaning & Combustion
  • Vacuum and clean main gas burner and chimney base
  • Inspect and clean blower motor
  • Clean furnace combustion chamber and ignition components
  • Inspect chimney base and flue pipe
  • Check burner manifold and burners
  • Check pilot flame quality and burner starting reliability
  • Inspect burner flame and make necessary adjustments
  • Blow Down Test Included
  • Check Sequence of Operations on safety controls on heating equipment.
Water, Piping & Circulation
  • Replace water-level gauge glass, gaskets and washers as needed during maintenance
  • Adjust aquastat or pressure control
  • Inspect and lubricate pumps, linkages and other moving parts
  • Check pipe and tubing connections, including Hartford loop and pressure-control pigtails
  • Inspect circulator pump(s), flow switches and automatic air valve(s)
  • Check automatic water feeder, backflow preventer and expansion tank
  • Inspect temperature-pressure gauge and low-water cutoff
Safety, Gas & Venting
  • Test for carbon-monoxide spillage
  • Perform gas-leak checks and check fuel-gas pressure
  • Test all safety controls
  • Inspect and test vent damper
  • Check blocked-vent and flame-rollout switches
  • Inspect all ducts
  • Check the quality and adequacy of primary and secondary air in the boiler room
Electrical, Controls & Sensors
  • Ensure electrical connections are secure and proper
  • Inspect and test switches, relays and transformers
  • Check flame sensor, including applicable thermocouple, thermopile, flame rod, UV scanner, photo cell, bimetallic sensor, diaphragm and capillary gas-filled sensor
  • Inspect control modules and printed circuit boards
  • Inspect and test Tekmar, Honeywell, Heat-Timer and Entech burner controls and monitors
  • Inspect indoor and outdoor temperature sensors
  • Adjust and/or program thermostat
Mechanical Components
  • Check boiler sections
  • Check valves, including pressure-regulating, pressure-relief, mixing, diverter, check, bleeder, zone, combination gas, flow, manual shutoff and drain valves
  • Check belts, shafts, vanes, impellers and bearings on pumps, fans and blowers
Service note: Maintenance needs vary by equipment type and condition. Heatspan can confirm which checks apply to your system and explain current warranty or plan coverage.
