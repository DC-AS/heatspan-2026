Designed for Natural-Gas Heating Customers

The plan is intended to help natural-gas customers keep boilers, furnaces, and related hot-water heating equipment maintained and protected. Heating systems are built for long service life, but even dependable equipment eventually needs maintenance and replacement parts.

Reduce Repair Surprises

A single heating repair can involve a service visit, replacement components, and labor. Plan coverage helps make eligible repairs more predictable.

Support Equipment Longevity

Seasonal maintenance and attention to developing problems can help keep equipment operating reliably.

Comfort & Safety

Emergency availability, maintenance, and carbon-monoxide awareness are combined with covered-parts protection.
