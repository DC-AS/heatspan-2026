When Should You Call for Heating Repair?

Changes in pressure, hot-water performance, sound or energy use can indicate that a boiler or heater needs professional attention.

  • Low water pressure
  • No hot water or insufficient heat
  • Banging or other unusual noises
  • Unexpected increases in energy use
  • Heating equipment that is no longer operating reliably

Professional Diagnosis Matters

Heating equipment includes fuel, combustion, water, electrical and safety components. Heatspan's trained technicians can identify the source of a problem and recommend an appropriate repair rather than relying on guesswork.
