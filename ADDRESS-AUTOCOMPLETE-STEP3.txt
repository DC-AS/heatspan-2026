HEATSPAN STEP 3 — SERVICE ADDRESS AUTOCOMPLETE

Status: Implemented.

- Vercel API route: /api/address-autocomplete
- Secret required: GEOAPIFY_API_KEY (configured in Vercel, not committed to source)
- Suggestions begin after 3 characters with a 350ms debounce.
- Results are restricted to U.S. addresses and biased toward Brooklyn/NYC.
- Customers can always type an address manually if suggestions are unavailable.
- Keyboard controls: Up/Down, Enter, Escape.
- No Network Solutions or live heatspan.com DNS changes are required.
