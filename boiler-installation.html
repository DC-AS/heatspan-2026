Boiler Installation & Replacement

When an older boiler becomes unreliable, inefficient, or repeatedly requires repair, replacement may offer a more dependable long-term solution.

Evaluate the Existing System

Review equipment condition, heating performance, recurring problems, and the needs of the home.

Select the Right Approach

Discuss practical replacement options based on the property, comfort goals, and project requirements.

Professional Installation

Complete installation, connect the new system, and verify proper operation before the project is finished.

When Replacement May Be Worth Considering

  • Frequent repairs are becoming more common or expensive
  • The system struggles to keep the home comfortable
  • Utility costs rise as performance declines
  • Leaks, shutdowns, pressure problems, or abnormal noises keep returning
  • The equipment is nearing the end of its practical service life

Financing Options Are Available

Larger heating projects can be difficult to plan for unexpectedly. Heatspan offers financing options that can help make qualified replacement projects more manageable.
