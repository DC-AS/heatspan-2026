Heatspan review flow update
- Financing and OFP external links open in new tabs.
- Removed five-star-only note under carousel.
- Replaced View All Heatspan Reviews with Leave A Review.
- Leave A Review opens a 1–5 star selector.
- Every rating continues to the user-provided Heatspan Google Maps listing in a new tab.
- 1–3 star selections also POST to /api/review-alert, which emails info@heatspan.com using RESEND_API_KEY.
- View Heatspan on Google now uses the user-provided Google Maps URL.

Important: The site cannot force Google sign-in. Google controls sign-in/review UI after the customer reaches Google.
